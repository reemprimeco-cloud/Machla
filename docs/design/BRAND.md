# HomeList — Brand sheet

**Mark:** Concept A "Roofline" — a cream roof over a caregiver and child, with
three list bars as the floor. Home, care, and list in one silhouette.

**Wordmark:** HomeList (Poppins Bold, -1.5% tracking) / هوم ليست (IBM Plex Sans Arabic SemiBold)

**Tagline:** One home. Every language. / بيت واحد. كل اللغات.

## Clear space & minimums

Clear space = ½ the mark's corner radius on all four sides.
Minimum size: **24 px** on screen, **12 mm** in print (mark alone).
With the flag ring: **34 mm** minimum, or the flags fill in.

## Don't

- Don't use the `tile` variant below 64 px — the bevel turns to mud.
- Don't fuse the flag ring into the mark. It is a detachable system element.
- Don't recolour the figures. Cream on green, or green on cream. Nothing else.
- Don't stretch, rotate, or add a drop shadow to the flat variant.
- Don't place the mark on a photo without a solid tile behind it.
